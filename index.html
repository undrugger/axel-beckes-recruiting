Athlete Bio

Axel is a multi-sport student-athlete from Alaska who competes at a high level in hockey, baseball, and bowling while maintaining a strong focus on academics and long-term collegiate goals in sports analytics and statistics.

In bowling, Axel has established himself as one of Alaska’s top junior competitors. He was a member of the 2025–2026 Alaska High School State Championship team from West High School and will compete at the 2026 National High School Team Championships. He rolled his first perfect game (300) in April 2026 and earned 1st Place All Events in the Division 1 Handicap category, along with 2nd Place All Events Scratch Division 1 in 2026. He is a six-time state scratch champion (formerly Pepsi) and a five-time Junior Gold qualifier.

On the ice, Axel is a key contributor for the Alaska Blue Devils AA program, where he served as captain of the 2025–2026 14U AA team. Under his leadership, the team finished as state tournament runners-up, and he led the tournament in goals during the 2026 season. His performance earned him selection to Team Alaska for the 2026 Arctic Winter Games. Axel also competed at the varsity level for West High School as a freshman, helping his team reach the 2026 state high school tournament.

On the baseball field, Axel continued to demonstrate his versatility and competitive edge. He was part of the 2025 American Legion A State Championship team with West Post 1 and also played for Grace Christian High School, where he led the team with a .606 batting average at season’s end. He plays multiple positions, including catcher, third base, first base, and pitcher, reflecting both athletic adaptability and game awareness.

Across all three sports, Axel is recognized for his competitive mindset, leadership, and ability to perform in high-pressure environments. He is pursuing opportunities to compete at the collegiate level while planning to major in mathematics or statistics, with a minor in communications, with the long-term goal of working in sports statistics or analytics.
