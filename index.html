Athlete Bio

Axel Beckes is a multi-sport student-athlete from Alaska who competes at a high level in hockey, baseball, and bowling while maintaining a strong academic focus and long-term collegiate goals in sports analytics and statistics.

In bowling, Axel is one of Alaska’s top junior competitors. He was a member of the 2025–2026 Alaska High School State Championship team at West High School and will compete at the 2026 National High School Team Championships. He has recorded a perfect game (300) and multiple top finishes at state-level competition, including Division 1 All Events honors.

In hockey, Axel serves as a captain for the Alaska Blue Devils AA program and was selected to Team Alaska for the Arctic Winter Games after leading his team in scoring at the state tournament. He has also competed at the varsity level for West High School.

In baseball, Axel plays multiple positions including catcher, third base, first base, and pitcher. He finished the season with a .606 batting average and contributed to a state championship team, demonstrating versatility and competitive consistency across all levels of play.
